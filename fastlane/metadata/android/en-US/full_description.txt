LogitNotes is a 100% local, privacy-respecting notes vault built with military-grade AES-256-GCM encryption.

Key Features:
• Zero Network / Zero Tracking: No analytics, no telemetry, no internet permission required. All data remains exclusively on your device.
• End-to-End Encryption: Notes and attachments are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using PBKDF2 key derivation (100,000 iterations).
• Biometric & PIN Security: Fast fingerprint/face unlock or custom numeric PIN with auto-lock security.
• Markdown Power: Full Markdown editor with live preview, headings, bold, italics, quotes, code, checklists, and dividers.
• Clean Organization: Folders, custom tags, pin important notes, and instant search across titles and content.
• Dark & Light Modes: Warm minimal ivory theme with seamless warm dark mode toggle.
• Open Formats: Direct export/import to standard Markdown (.md) and encrypted ZIP archives.
• Battery & RAM Optimized: Highly efficient in-memory caching and debounced disk writes.
