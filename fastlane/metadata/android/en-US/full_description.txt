LogitNotes is an open-source, offline-first secure personal notes vault.

Key Features:
- 100% Local Storage: Your notes stay on your device. Zero cloud dependency, zero trackers.
- Military-Grade Encryption: AES-256-GCM encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation (100,000 iterations).
- Native Biometrics: Fingerprint and facial recognition unlock using Android BiometricPrompt.
- Standard Markdown: Full Markdown (.md) support with task lists, headings, and formatting.
- Encrypted Attachments: Secure local storage for pictures and documents.
- Export & Backup: Export single notes as plain .md or export the entire vault as a .zip bundle.
- User-Defined Master Key: No default passwords. Every user sets their unique passphrase.
