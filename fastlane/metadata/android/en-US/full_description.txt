LogitNotes is a zero-trust, completely offline personal note-taking app and security vault:

• 100% Offline & Private: No cloud, no analytics, no external trackers. The app does not declare or request the INTERNET permission.
• AES-256 GCM Encryption: Notes, titles, contents, and attachments are strongly encrypted on device.
• Biometric & PIN Security: Secure your vault with fingerprint, face unlock, or Master PIN with automatic lock on pause.
• Folders & Tag Taxonomy: Organize notes in folders with full renaming support and tag indexing.
• Minimalist Search: Fast, inline search bar on the home screen to find notes across titles and content.
• Dark & Light Modes: Warm dark theme designed to reduce eye strain and conserve OLED battery.
• Live Markdown Editor: WYSIWYG live rendering, formatting toolbar, checklists, code blocks, and math/tables support.
• Import & Export: Export directly to standard Markdown (.md) or whole-vault ZIP archives.
• Battery & Memory Optimized: Memory-efficient decryption caching and lightweight SQLite storage.
